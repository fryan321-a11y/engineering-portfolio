Featured field project

70-Foot Limestone Bypass Excavation

Project Lead · Central Texas · Multi-season

A multi-season underground excavation following a strong airflow-bearing fracture toward known cave passage. The project required adapting excavation, haulage, and surveying methods as the working face moved farther from the entrance.

Engineering / field scope

Interpreted fractures, faults, airflow, and observed geology to guide excavation direction; organized work at the face; used rotary drilling, demolition hammers, and rock-fracturing methods; and coordinated rigging, hoisting, spoil removal, and material haulage in confined underground conditions.

Documentation

Produced plan/profile mapping of the excavation and surrounding cave, including changes across the 2024–2025 phases and the relationship between the dig, filled cracks, washout, and airflow.
